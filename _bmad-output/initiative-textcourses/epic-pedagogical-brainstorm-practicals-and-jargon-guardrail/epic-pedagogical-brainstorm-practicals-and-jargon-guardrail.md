# Epic 8: Pedagogical Empathy, Autonomous Multi-Path Brainstorming & AI Practical Verification

**Initiative**: `initiative-textcourses`

**Status**: completed  
**Covers**: FR-34, FR-35, FR-36, FR-37  

## Executive Summary
This epic addresses vital user feedback to transform TextCourses into an intensely empathetic, autonomously decisive, and practically rigorous educational platform:
1. **Beginner-to-Core Pedagogical Adaptation (FR-34)**: Deeply honors beginner status across research (prioritizing friction hunter and intuitive metaphorist hats), course structure (foundational prerequisites, smaller increments), teaching (Hypatia leadership and cognitive load pacing), and testing.
2. **Autonomous Multi-Path Curriculum Brainstorming & Hat Validation (FR-35)**: Completely eliminates interactive stalling/asking "how do you want this course created?". Autonomously synthesizes 4 distinct pedagogical architectures (Foundational First-Principles, Hands-on Project-First, Problem-Driven Diagnostic, Intuitive Metaphor-First), submits them to Universal Critical Hats for validation, debates tradeoffs in the Triad, and directly creates the optimal hybrid course.
3. **In-Loop Practical Instruction & AI Practical Evaluation (FR-36)**: Integrates the practicals agent (Daedalus / `practical_evaluator.py`) directly into the teaching loop when practicals are requested, scaffolding runnable challenges in `lessons/lesson-XX/practical.md`, and evaluating submissions using AI against correctness invariants and edge cases.
4. **Zero-Unexplained-Jargon & Human-First Readability Guardrail (FR-37)**: Guarantees plain English readability across notes and teaching, strictly forbidding ungrounded jargon unless explained in a previous chapter or demystified with an intuitive analogy upon introduction.
